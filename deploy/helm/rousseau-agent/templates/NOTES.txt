⚠  replicaCount={{ .Values.replicaCount }} but the chart cannot verify
   your state driver is postgres. Multi-replica installs REQUIRE
   state.driver=postgres — otherwise every pod runs its own SQLite
   and session state does NOT cross pods (users see random gaps
   depending on which pod they land on).

   Configure via values.yaml:

     config:
       state:
         driver: postgres
         dsn: postgres://user:pass@host:5432/rousseau?sslmode=require

   See ROADMAP §2.4 for the HA architecture notes.
{{- end }}
